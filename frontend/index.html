🛡️ How to Protect Yourself from Phishing

1
Always verify the sender's email domain — look for subtle misspellings like "paypa1.com"
2
Hover over links before clicking — the real URL appears in the bottom of your browser
3
Legitimate companies never ask for passwords, SSN, or CVV via email
4
Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on all important accounts
5
When in doubt, go directly to the official website — don't click email links
6
Use a password manager and unique passwords for every site
7
Keep your browser and OS updated — patches fix known security holes
